# Gear Progression — All Arcs

> Adding gear for a new arc? Also read [`../DESIGN_LESSONS.md`](../DESIGN_LESSONS.md) — Rules 4.1–4.3 govern gear progression, starter-kit coverage, and cross-arc type counters.


**Design rule:** every arc ships a visibly cooler tier of gear. Names, stats, and moves all climb. The Crown Treasure is the peak of its arc and grants a **permanent bonus move** on top of gear-derived moves.

## Element balance across treasure bonus moves

| Treasure | Element | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| 💎 Gem of Wisdom | ✨ magic | Mountain water enemies |
| 🗡️ Blade of Bravery | 💨 wind | Beach magic enemies |
| 🛡️ Shield of Kindness | 💧 water (heal) | Party support in any arc |
| 🔥 Fire of Courage | 🔥 fire | Volcano earth enemies |
| 🌟 Star of Friendship | ✨ magic | Endgame / Mornox |

Element coverage in bonus moves: magic ×2, wind ×1, water ×1 (heal), fire ×1. Kid ends with 5 stacking bonus moves on top of their 4 gear moves = **9 total moves at endgame**, spread across every type.

With 2× type advantage, late-game hits can top 75 damage per swing.

## Why this design works for kids

- **Each adventure = clear stat jump.** Numbers go up. Kids notice.
- **Names sell it.** "Frost Fang Sword" > "Weapon Mk 3". Flavor carries progression.
- **Choice reinforcement.** How you beat the boss determines what you get. Replay value.
- **Type strategy grows.** Early earth → mid water/wind → late fire/magic. Loadout feel shifts.
- **Treasure moves stack forever.** By end-game James has 5 extra moves on top of his 4 gear moves — he feels like a walking arsenal.
