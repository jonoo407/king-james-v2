# King James 2 — Plot Bible

> Every arc should feel like a chapter in ONE story, not a standalone adventure. This doc is that story: who Mornox is, why the Treasures matter, and how Mornox's shadow grows across every arc until James meets him face-to-face.

> **DOCS NOTE (May 2026):** the Beach section originally planned a Forgotten Mermaid (Sirena) villain. That version was never built — shipped beach uses **Drifter the Healer**, an isolated woman driven into a sea cove by Mornox's lies. §4 Arc 3 below has been updated to reflect what shipped. The original Sirena spec is preserved in [`arcs/beach.md`](arcs/beach.md) for historical reference but is not canonical.

## 1. The elevator pitch (kid-accessible, 30 seconds)

**A long time ago, the great crown of the kingdom got broken into 5 magical Treasures and scattered across the land. Each Treasure keeps one part of the kingdom safe.**

**A cursed wizard named MORNOX wants all 5 Treasures. If he gets them, he can break his curse — but the kingdom would crumble with it.**

**The crown picked a kid — James — to find the Treasures first. Mornox is racing him. And Mornox knows how to whisper into the crown when he wants to.**

Every kid should understand this after Scene 1 of Forest.

## 2. Mornox — full villain dossier

### Origin (optional flashback/dream content)
Long ago Mornox was a royal wizard. Impatient, clever, reckless — exactly what James is now. He cast the **Crown Steal Spell** to take the throne for himself. The spell *backfired* — it shattered the crown into 5 Treasures, scattered them, and **cursed Mornox into an ageless, lonely, grouchy wizard who can never die and never rest.**

He's been alive for 400 years. Everyone he knew is dead. Every friend. Every enemy. He's SICK of it.

### What he wants
**To end his curse.** Only one thing can do that: reuniting all 5 Treasures and undoing the original spell.

He can't touch them himself — the curse prevents it. **He needs someone to collect them for him.** Then he plans to take them at the last moment.

That "someone" is James.

### What he does
- **Waits.** He's been waiting 400 years. He has patience — the one thing James doesn't.
- **Watches.** He can sometimes HIJACK the crown's voice channel. When he does, it's scary and sudden.
- **Corrupts.** He touches local bosses subtly — whispering into their fears, amplifying grudges. Each arc's mini-villain has been nudged by Mornox.
- **Never lies outright** — he just leaves out the worst parts. (Classic unreliable narrator.)

### Mirror to James
Both impatient schemers. James is what Mornox was. Mornox is what James could become. This parallel runs through **every arc**:
- Forest: James must LEARN PATIENCE (riddle tree). Mornox couldn't.
- Mountain: James must LISTEN before swinging. Mornox never listened.
- Beach: James must CARE about others. Mornox stopped caring.
- Desert: James must PERSIST with hope. Mornox gave up on hope centuries ago.
- Volcano: James must FORGIVE. Mornox could never forgive himself.

**Every arc's lesson is something Mornox failed to learn.** That's the spine.

### Voice / style
- **Dry, tired, old-man sarcasm.** Not mustache-twirling evil.
- **Always tired.** He says "oh my back" and "kids these days" unironically.
- **Occasionally slips into actual despair.** When he does, the kid sees the person under the curse.
- **Calls James "boy" or "little king" or "my errand-runner."**
- **Never angry in words** — his anger shows in the world (storms, withered plants).

Sample lines:
- *"Oh look, you found ONE. The Gem of Wisdom. Clever. How BORING of you to succeed."*
- *"Keep going, boy. Every treasure you pick up saves me walking to it."*
- *"Do you know what 400 years feels like, little king? I\'ll tell you when you get here."*
- *"You\'re clever. I was clever too. Turned out great for me, didn\'t it."*

## 3. The 5 Crown Treasures — what they actually DO

In-fiction, the 5 Treasures are the 5 pieces of the broken royal crown, each imbued with one royal virtue. **Each Treasure protects one part of the kingdom.** When a Treasure is missing for too long, that part of the land starts to decay.

| Treasure | Virtue | Protects | If Mornox takes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 💎 Gem of Wisdom | wisdom | Forest (keeps plants growing) | Forest begins to wither |
| 🗡️ Blade of Bravery | bravery | Mountain (keeps villagers safe from the curse) | Avalanches, terror returns |
| 🛡️ Shield of Kindness | kindness | Beach / Shore (sea stays gentle) | Storms, drownings |
| 🔥 Fire of Courage | courage | Desert (sun is warm, not deadly) | Desert becomes lethal |
| 🌟 Star of Friendship | friendship | Volcano + kingdom-wide (binds everyone together) | Isolation, civil collapse |

James doesn\'t know all of this at the start — the Crown reveals what each Treasure does as it\'s recovered.

**Why James collects them BEFORE Mornox:** the Crown summoned him specifically because Mornox can\'t touch the Treasures. But Mornox is patient. If James takes too long, the kingdom falls anyway.

**The twist at the end:** Mornox doesn\'t plan to destroy the Treasures — he wants to UNDO them, which requires reuniting them on his terms. He intercepts James at Volcano when all 5 are together.

## 4. Each arc\'s local-to-global thread

Every arc has a **local villain** AND a **Mornox thread**. The local villain gives the arc its standalone story; the Mornox thread makes it feel like part of the bigger plot.

### Arc 1 — Forest (✅ shipped)
- **Local villain:** Hoarder Troll (keeps the Gem buried in his cave)
- **Local lesson for James:** Patience beats brute force (Riddle Tree)
- **Mornox thread:**
  - The Troll muttered "an old wizard told me no one would notice if I hid it forever." → that wizard was Mornox.
  - After beating Troll, a dusty scroll falls out of his treasure pile: *"Thank you, Troll. Keep it safe forever. — M."*
  - Crown in reward scene: *"Huh. That handwriting. That\'s... no, it couldn\'t be. Never mind."*
- **Mornox\'s first whisper** — between Forest end and Mountain start, Mornox cuts into the crown voice briefly: *"One down. Only four to go, little king. No rush."*

### Arc 2 — Mountain (✅ shipped)
- **Local villain:** Glimmer the Wraith (wanted to be remembered)
- **Local lesson:** Scary isn\'t the same as bad; listen before swinging
- **Mornox thread:**
  - In the Wraith\'s reveal scene, add one line: *"A wizard visited me once. Ages ago. Told me no one would ever sing about me. Made me angry. Made me do this."*
  - After the arc, Mornox breaks the crown voice again: *"The Wraith was a friend of mine, once. I didn\'t kill him. I just TOLD him nobody cared. He did the rest."*
- **Stakes rise** — back at the castle, **a vine has withered overnight**. Crown: *"That shouldn\'t be possible now — the Gem is BACK. Unless... Mornox is already pulling power from somewhere else."*

### Arc 3 — Beach (✅ shipped — uses Drifter, not Sirena)
- **Local villain:** **Drifter** — once a healer who tended sailors for thirty years. Mornox came through, told the village she was cursing the sea and poisoning the fish. They believed him. She's been alone in a sea cove for 40+ years, bitter and dangerous.
- **Local lesson:** Kindness means looking at things from the other side. The "monster" is a person Mornox lied about; the cure is bringing her back into the village.
- **Mornox thread:** This is **the same trick he played on the Wraith** — plant one small lie about someone, let the world do the rest. Kid sees the pattern: Mornox doesn't kill people, he isolates them. The shipped lore tablet (`knows_drifter_past` flag) reveals it explicitly. The redemption beat: Drifter realizes she could come back, and the village widow needs a healer.
- **Kindness flags** (used by Volcano's Listen-path predicate): `serpent_friend` (cut the net, freed baby sea serpents) OR `knows_drifter_past` (read the ruins tablet that exposes Mornox's role).

### Arc 4 — Desert (🔜 planned)
- **Local villain:** A twin — Mornox\'s **former apprentice**, now a sun-witch who went rogue. She ALSO wants to un-curse the sun (she\'s trying to fix the damage Mornox did). Unclear if friend or foe.
- **Local lesson:** Persistence + hope. Don\'t give up even when the path is long.
- **Mornox thread:** This apprentice was kind once. Mornox broke her heart. She\'s not the villain — she\'s trying to help, in a broken way. James can ally with her and get the Fire of Courage together.
- **Mornox intervention:** he appears BRIEFLY in person for the first time — a ghost-projection in the Desert shrine. Kid sees him! He just watches. Doesn\'t attack. Leaves a cryptic line: *"You\'re almost done. Good boy."*
- **Stakes rise:** the kingdom is visibly decaying now. Villagers can feel it.

### Arc 5 — Volcano (🔜 planned)
- **Local villain:** Mornox himself + his corrupted dragon guardian.
- This IS the final confrontation. The volcano is his lair.
- **Local lesson:** Forgiveness. James must see Mornox not as a monster but as a man who made one bad choice and has been paying for it for 400 years.
- **Mornox thread:** no subtlety left. Kid arrives with all 5 Treasures. Mornox tries to TAKE them. Boss fight. But the endgame offers a CHOICE:
  - **Attack option:** beat Mornox with the full arsenal. Curse breaks, Mornox dies old. Bittersweet.
  - **Redemption option (harder, requires all 5 arc-lessons):** listen, then help Mornox un-curse himself + heal the kingdom. Everyone lives. Mornox walks off into the sunset, crown whole.

## 5. Mornox\'s interventions — pacing and delivery

### Intervention beats (between arcs)

After each arc completes and kid returns to the castle, Mornox appears briefly. Not a battle — a narrative intrusion. Build dread.

| # | When | Intervention type | Tone | Sample line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | After Forest | Crown voice hijack (scary — crown glitches harder, Mornox speaks instead) | Dismissive | *"Oh look. You found ONE. The Gem. How... boring of you to succeed."* |
| 2 | After Mountain | Scroll delivered — someone left it on the throne overnight | Cold, amused | *"The Wraith was a friend of mine once. I told him no one cared. He did the rest. Welcome to my hobby."* |
| 3 | After Beach | Physical gift (still-living bee in a glass jar appears in castle) | Threatening | *"Proof I can still reach inside your walls, little king. I\'m patient. You\'re not."* |
| 4 | After Desert | Ghost projection appears at castle briefly — kid SEES him for the first time | Tired, almost weary | *"You\'re almost done. Good boy. I\'ve been waiting 400 years. I can wait a week more."* |
| 5 | Entering Volcano | Full confrontation begins | All of the above + vulnerability leaks | *"Welcome home, little king. I\'ve got tea."* |

### Technical implementation

Add a new dialogue scene type or mechanism: **between arcs at the castle, after rewarding the Treasure, before kid regains control, a Mornox intervention plays automatically.**

- Creates a **Mornox dialogue pool** in `data/crown-dialogue.js` (new context `mornox_intervention`, tagged by arc count).
- The intervention is ONE dialogue scene per arc completion. Kid can\'t skip it.
- Uses a special styled speech bubble (purple/black, glitchy border) to signal "this is NOT the crown."
- After intervention, Crown reboots and apologizes: *"Sorry. He does that. Getting harder to block him out."*

### Crown\'s emotional arc about Mornox
The Crown is hiding something. At the START, Crown pretends not to know Mornox. As more treasures come back, Crown gets clearer AND more honest. By arc 4 we learn: **the crown is what\'s LEFT of Mornox\'s old mentor — a wizard who tried to stop him 400 years ago and got trapped in the crown.** Not a replacement — an eternal punishment Mornox inflicted on his own teacher.

That twist doesn\'t need to land early. Plant seeds in arc 3 (crown slips on a name), reveal at arc 5.

The kid should feel the stakes growing. Not just "one more treasure to collect" but "if I don\'t do this, everything breaks."

## 7. The final confrontation — two paths

At the top of Volcano:

**Setup:** James arrives with all 5 Treasures. Mornox is waiting. He\'s set up an old kettle. There are two chairs.

**Mornox\'s move:** he tries to take the Treasures. James can resist (fight) or sit down (talk).

**Path A: Fight**
- Three-phase boss battle (Mornox uses all 5 types across phases)
- Uses every mechanic (scrolls, ally swaps, gear loadout per phase)
- Victory: curse breaks, Mornox crumbles to dust peacefully
- **Bittersweet ending** — "he finally got to rest"

**Path B: Listen (harder — requires having chosen the "kind" path in arcs 3 + 4)**
- James sits down. Mornox tells the real story (flashback).
- Shared moment of mutual recognition.
- James offers to help un-curse instead of steal-and-go
- Together they reunite treasures peacefully. Curse breaks, Mornox de-ages 50 years (still old, but human again), walks away into the sunset.
- **Redemption ending** — "he finally got to leave."

Both endings are valid. Both end with the kid becoming the true king.

## 10. What this doc guarantees

- The plot makes sense at every level: a 7yo understands "bad wizard wants treasures," the emotionally-tuned kid feels the escalating dread, the completionist kid reads lore and gets the twist.
- Every arc feels connected to the main story, not isolated.
- Mornox has a presence in EVERY arc — not just endgame.
- The final confrontation is earned by 5 arcs of thematic setup.
- The mirror theme (James and Mornox) gives the game emotional weight without being heavy.

### Design rule (add to DESIGN_LESSONS.md)
**Rule 6.5 · Main villain must appear in EVERY arc, not just endgame.**
- Rule: even if Mornox isn't a boss in an arc, he must be *felt* — via a scroll, a message, a hijacked crown voice, a physical gift at the castle. Every arc ending needs a Mornox beat.
- Checklist when authoring a new arc: ☐ Mornox thread included (intervention beat at arc end + one in-arc hint).
- Why: without ongoing villain presence, arcs feel like standalone episodes. Presence builds dread and makes the finale land.
